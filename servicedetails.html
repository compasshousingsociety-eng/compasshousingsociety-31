Comprehensive Management for Modern Real Estate

At Compass Housing Society, our Property Management & Leasing service is designed to maximize your property's value while minimizing hassle. From tenant screening to rent collection, maintenance coordination to legal compliance — we handle every detail with professional care and transparent communication.

What's Included

  • Tenant Acquisition & Screening: Rigorous background, credit, and rental history verification.
  • Lease Agreement & Legal Compliance: RERA-compliant documentation and legal protection.
  • Maintenance & Repairs: 24/7 emergency support and preventive maintenance scheduling.
  • Rent Collection & Financial Reporting: Monthly statements, online payments, and tax-ready summaries.
  • Eviction & Dispute Resolution: Professional legal assistance if needed, protecting your investment.

Why Choose Our Leasing Experts?

Our dedicated leasing team combines local market intelligence with innovative marketing strategies. We advertise across premium portals, arrange virtual/ physical tours, and negotiate terms that align with current market dynamics. With an average vacancy reduction of 35%, we ensure your property stays profitable year-round.
