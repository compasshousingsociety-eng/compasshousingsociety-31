FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries about our properties and services.

What types of properties does Compass Housing offer?
We offer a diverse range including luxury apartments, premium villas, gated communities, and commercial office spaces across prime locations in Hyderabad and surrounding areas.
Are all your projects RERA approved?
Yes, every Compass Housing project is fully RERA registered with complete transparency in documentation, pricing, and delivery timelines. RERA numbers are displayed on all project pages.
Can I schedule a site visit before booking?
Absolutely! We encourage site visits. Please fill out the contact form or call our sales team to book a personalized tour of any of our ongoing projects at your convenience.
What financing options are available?
We have tie-ups with major banks and housing finance companies offering home loans up to 90% of the property value. Our team will assist you throughout the loan process.
Do you provide post-purchase support?
Yes, we have a dedicated customer care team that assists with registration, handover, maintenance, and any post-purchase queries for up to 5 years after delivery.
