Building More Than Homes

For over a decade, Compass Housing Society has been redefining urban living with integrity, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to community well-being.

Our Journey

A Legacy of Trust & Excellence

Founded in 2012, Compass Housing Society began with a simple yet powerful vision: to create living spaces that foster genuine human connection. What started as a small team of passionate architects and real estate professionals has grown into one of the most trusted names in premium residential and commercial developments.

Over the past 12 years, we've delivered over 3,250 homes across 28+ successful projects, maintaining a remarkable 98% customer satisfaction rate. Our approach combines cutting-edge design, sustainable practices, and transparent processes — ensuring every homeowner feels confident and cared for.

Today, Compass Housing stands as a beacon of quality and reliability, with a growing portfolio of luxury apartments, eco-friendly villas, and smart commercial spaces. Yet our mission remains unchanged: to make the journey of finding your perfect home as meaningful as the destination itself.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional living environments that blend architectural brilliance with functional comfort, ensuring every homeowner experiences transparency, quality, and lasting value in every interaction.

Our Vision

To become India's most admired real estate brand, recognized for creating sustainable, tech-enabled communities that set new benchmarks in design, trust, and resident well-being.
